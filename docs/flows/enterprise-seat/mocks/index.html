Enterprise Near AI Gateway: seats in an org

One member's Mac and one server seat through the life of an org, from the design in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-enterprise-gateway-design.md. Each frame's title is a chart state. The member frames show only the controls the model gives for that state.

  1. Joining never carries your Automatic over. Holds.
  2. Your traces leave only by your act. Holds.
  3. If the org forbids Automatic, nothing of yours goes automatically. Holds after fix: the claim carries the policy.
  4. Sharing off means no new credit. Holds.
  5. Removal stops uploads, including one already in flight. Holds.
  6. Leaving the NEAR AI org stops uploads by your next sign-in. Holds.
  7. You can take back what you gave the org. Holds.
  8. What you earned personally stays withdrawable. Holds after fix: Before you joined.
  9. Servers share only under the org's consent. Holds.
  10. The org closes cleanly. Holds.
  11. A submitted trace eventually lands. Holds, with two conditions you added: policy settles, and your sign-in lasts.

What the model assumes

  • Org identity is NEAR AI organization membership (decision 1); credit goes to the org only (decision 3); one enrolment per Mac, except a withdraw-only personal credential for "Before you joined" (decision 4, change B).
  • Removal opens a closing window that allows only Withdraw (decision 6). Its length is not modelled.
  • Org sessions do not slide; an expired one is renewed only by a fresh membership check (spec 5.3).
  • Automatic is enforced on the Mac and by the issuer: the claim carries the policy digest and how the trace was sent (change A). Sharing off, removal, suspension and a lapsed sign-in are checked again when the upload arrives.
  • In an org, the Mac sends nothing while it is signed out (change C).
  • One member, one server, one trace at a time each.
