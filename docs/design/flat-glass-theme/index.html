Flat glass: a light-tint and a dark-tint theme

The getone.one components rebuilt with Trace Commons content. The light tint uses the site's own values unchanged. The dark tint keeps the same navy hue, raises the veil and lowers the rims. Click and hover the components: they respond as the originals do. Contrast is worked out for white text over the lightest and darkest point of each backdrop.

Rulings this would reopen

Brand purple (D3)

The source has no accent colour: a selected item is a solid white pill with near-black text. Use the "Selected state" switch above to see the brand purple in that role.

Hover from #1146

In the source, hover draws a 1px white ring at 50% and keeps the fill. #1146 changes the fill instead (controlHover, cardHover). On wells, as in the answer rows and the Run button, the source darkens the fill as well.

WCAG floors on light grounds

White text on the light tint falls below 4.5:1 over bright scenes such as Sky and the light desktop. The source relies on a text shadow, which the contrast tests do not count. The dark tint clears 4.5:1 on every backdrop shown.

Type sizes

The source sets 14px medium for body and 15px for titles. Our type steps are macOS text styles (body 13, title3 15), so 14 has no matching style. Moving to it means overriding Dynamic Type sizes or choosing the nearest style.

Radii

Source radii are 24, 20, 18, 16, 14, 11 and 6, against ours of 16, 14, 12, 8 and 6. Larger radii suit the flat look but take room in dense panes like the tree and the inspector.

Native material

On macOS, panes use Liquid Glass and our tokens only veil it. The source paints its own fill, rims and drop shadow. Either the veil carries the tint and the rims become overlays, or panes stop using the native material.
