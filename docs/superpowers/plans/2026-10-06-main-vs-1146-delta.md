## How this was captured, and its limits

**macOS main.** This is a Debug build, `cargo build -p trace-commons-contributor-ffi` plus `macos/scripts/make-app-bundle.sh`. It was launched with `TRACE_COMMONS_SAMPLE=normalDay`, which draws the recorded samples in `macos/Sources/TCShellCore/DataContract/RecordedSamples/`. It ran against a throwaway state directory set up the way `run-demo.sh` sets one up, with `TRACE_COMMONS_APPEARANCE=dark|light`.

- Windows were captured from the running app with `screencapture -l`. They are 1x captures from a non-Retina display, at the app's 1060×760 window.
- **The bundle needed one fix to run.** `make-app-bundle.sh` does not copy the `TraceCommons_TCShellCore.bundle` resource bundle into the .app.
  - With `TRACE_COMMONS_SAMPLE` set, the app traps at launch: `Bundle.module` asserts from `SampleDaemonData.recordedFile`.
  - I copied the bundle in by hand. This is worth fixing in the script.
- The app's `DebugScreenshot` facility (`TRACE_COMMONS_SCREENSHOT_DIR`) was also run. Its `ImageRenderer` path cannot draw Liquid Glass or AppKit-backed views:
  - The preview sheet comes out as the "unsupported view" placeholder.
  - The withdrawal confirmation's capsules clip.
  - Those images are not used as evidence of the design. The Settings sections it rendered are used for text and structure only.
- **First run.** I launched with no daemon settings, so the daemon starts only when Folders or Tools commits, as on a real first install. `HOME` pointed at a throwaway home holding a fixture session, so the real `~/.claude` was never read.
- **One local harness patch.** The Folders/Tools mode pickers are pop-up menus, and the background UI driver cannot open them. To get past them I made a temporary edit to `ToolAnswerRow.swift`, gated on `TC_DELTA_AUTOANSWER=1`, that pre-selects "Watch this folder" for each found tool.
  - The edit was reverted before commit. It is not in this branch.
  - It changes only which answer is selected. Where the passkey sheets open is untouched.
- The screen locked partway through the light-mode first-run pass, so no light-mode passkey sheet was captured.

**#1146.** Its Vite dev frontend ran in headless Chromium at 1060×760, 2x. #1146 has no mock layer of its own, so I wrote a Tauri IPC shim (`window.__TAURI_INTERNALS__`).

- The shim answers daemon reads from **the same macOS recorded samples** (`normalDay`), which keeps the data on both sides identical.
- It answers #1146's pure copy commands with a small Rust program. That program calls the same `trace_commons_contributor` copy functions the Tauri commands call, built from #1146's own tree.
- So the words on screen are the core's words, not placeholders.
- Commands it did not answer show #1146's own error state; they are listed under "Not captured".
- These are browser captures, not the Tauri window: there is no native vibrancy (`tc-native` is off), and the page draws its scene background.

**#1030.** These came from the `origin/ftux` Storybook stories (`FtuxPage`, `PasskeyChoose`, `PasskeySystemSignIn`, `PasskeyWelcomeBack`, `ReturningUser`, `JoinWithPasskeyCard`), clicked through P-1 to P-5 in context.

The macOS app already takes #1146's overall structure:

- toolbar view menu and graph toggle;
- Home / Inference / Traces tabs;
- breadcrumb;
- three panes with the map and inspector;
- glass Settings modal;
- legend cells;
- watch switch;
- submit pills;
- row-off at 0.6.

The left pane is a fixed 360pt, the inspector is 300pt, and opening the map or the inspector grows the window rightwards without resizing the other panes, all as Ron ruled.

What separates the two most visibly, in order:

1. **Text breaking inside buttons and pills at the default window.**
   - Inference: "Re-fresh", "Add ac-count", and "Launch managed session" stacked five lines tall.
   - Session inspector: "Con-tribute".
   - History legend: "Pend-ing" and "Con-trib-uted".
   - Folder decisions: "Submit all as…".
2. **The inspector is stacked with offer and first-contribution cards** above whatever is selected, on every tab. #1146's inspector shows only the selection.
3. **The graph footer** lacks #1146's zoom, focus and jump-to-now controls and its range pill.
4. **On macOS 26 the panes and floating controls are Liquid Glass.** #1146's pane gradient, veil and edge are not drawn, and a 10pt inset surrounds the panes.
5. **Home and History use their own tiles, wells and row layouts** instead of #1146's StatCard, the SUBMISSIONS/PROJECT grouping and history rows.
6. **Token breaches of explicit rulings:**
   - the watch-switch knob has a dark ring;
   - the placeholder ink is #B4B4BC rather than 30% white;
   - the selection blue (#2F6AC0 vs #3a7bd5) and the eyebrow weight (600 vs 700) differ.

## Passkey-sheet placement (P-1..P-7)

### What main does

`Create passkey` on Join records a choice if the daemon is not running yet (`mac:TraceCommonsApp/Views/FirstRun/JoinScreen.swift:202-214`, `JoinScreenLayout.passkeyOpensNow`). Folders or Tools commits `FirstRunCall.openPasskeySheets` (`mac:TCShellCore/FirstRun/FirstRunPlan.swift:109-114, 163`). The sheets then open over whatever step comes next.

| Case | Where P-1 opens on main | Capture |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh install, Quick | over **Uses**, after Folders → Continue | `passkey-P1-quick-uses.png` |
| Fresh install, Custom | over **Rules**, after Tools → Continue | `passkey-P1-custom-rules.png` |
| Daemon already running (first run re-entered, or roots pre-seeded) | over **Join**, at once | `passkey-P1-join-daemon-running.png` |

The Join card after the choice reads "You'll create your passkey once watching starts." with an Undo, and Continue replaces "Skip: watch only" (`passkey-join-choice.png`, left).

### What #1030 does

Create passkey on Join opens P-1 over Join immediately (`origin/ftux:…/features/ftux/ftux-page.tsx:276-322`), and the whole ceremony runs there:

- P-1 Choose and P-2 Name;
- P-3/P-4, the simulated system "Save a passkey?" and Touch ID sheets;
- P-5 Verify;
- P-6, the simulated system sign-in for "Use existing";
- P-7 Welcome back, for a returning user, also over Join.

Captures: `passkey-P1-*.png` (right side), `passkey-P2.png`, `passkey-P3-P4-ftux-only.png`, `passkey-P5-P6-ftux-only.png`, `passkey-P7-ftux-only.png`.

### Differences visible in the pairs

1. **Context.** Whichever step it opens over, the macOS scrim darkens and blurs the step to near-black (`passkey-P1-quick-uses.png`, left). Over Uses or Rules you cannot tell which step you are on. #1030's scrim keeps the Join card faintly readable behind the popup. So today the placement on main mostly reads as "a popup appeared after I pressed Continue", not as "a popup over Uses".
2. **Size.** The macOS popup is about 360pt wide, with content-hugging buttons. #1030's is about 380px, with full-width stacked buttons (primary purple, secondary glass) and a larger icon.
3. **Content** matches: titles, button labels, footnotes, P-2's field and warning, and the corner buttons (Close on P-1; Back and Close on P-2).
4. **Ordering.** On main a fresh-install person meets the passkey popup **after** choosing folders, with the step indicator already on Uses or Rules. Closing it leaves them on that step with the account still unmade, and the Join card's "once watching starts" promise kept. In #1030 the popup answers the Create passkey click directly.

### Not captured on main

- **P-3, P-4 and P-6** are real system sheets, raised by `NativePasskeyCoordinator`. Raising them means creating a passkey or account, which I did not do.
- **P-5 Verify** comes only after a real ceremony.
- **P-7** needs a stored passkey.
- The **light-mode** passkey sheets: the screen locked mid-run.

### Recommendation for Ron

Open the sheets **over Join**, as #1030 does. Main already draws them over Join correctly whenever the daemon is running (`passkey-P1-join-daemon-running.png`). The deferred placement exists only because the ceremony completes with a daemon that starts at the Folders/Tools commit.

The two ways to get there:

- **(a) Start the daemon for the ceremony while on Join.** For example, start it without roots, or with roots unset, and let Folders write them later. Then `passkeyOpensNow` is true on a fresh install too. This needs an engineering check that the daemon can bind an account before any roots are declared.
- **(b) If (a) is not feasible, keep the deferral but open the sheets over the step that committed (Folders or Tools) before advancing.** Pair that with a lighter scrim, so the step stays readable and the popup reads as the result of Continue.

Either way, the popup's size and button layout should follow #1030: full-width stacked buttons, about 380 wide. Ron to decide; nothing was changed.
