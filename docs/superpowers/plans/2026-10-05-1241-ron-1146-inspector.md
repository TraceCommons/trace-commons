Every daemon call already exists on #1241 except the turn index bridge.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 (`macos/`), TCDesign, the Rust contributor core and its FFI, XCTest. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** Ron's #1146 (`origin/claude/tc-monitor-frontend-refactor-8ecf22`, `tauri-desktop/frontend/src/app/monitor/monitor-shell.tsx`, `features/waiting/{waiting-page.tsx,components/*}`, `features/history/*`, `app/monitor/home-view.tsx`); the research findings (2026-10-05) reproduced in Global Constraints; owner decisions of 2026-10-05.
