# Near.ai v0.18.2

Start OpenCode with your NEAR AI sign-in, and see the estimate as soon as the app starts.

- On macOS, the NEAR AI card on the Private AI tab can start OpenCode when it is installed, as it already can Claude Code and Codex (#1454).
- The estimate of what your traces could earn appears as soon as the app starts, rather than hours later (#1450).
- Two notices about automatic contributing are no longer shown (#1453).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1450, #1453 and #1454.
