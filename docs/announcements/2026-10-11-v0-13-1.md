# Near.ai v0.13.1

A maintenance release for the macOS app, based on v0.13.0.

- Notices above the tabs scroll once they fill their space. With many watched folders, one notice per folder used to push the tabs and the rest of the window out of view.
- "Dismiss all" acknowledges every notice that has its own "Got it", in one press.
- The menu-bar panel has "Open Near.ai", which brings the main window back.
- In Light appearance the window's background is more opaque, so less of the desktop shows through behind text.
