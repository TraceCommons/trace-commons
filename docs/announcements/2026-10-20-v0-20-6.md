# Near.ai v0.20.6

The macOS app no longer quits when the edge rail opens or closes, and it takes the NEAR AI accent.

- On macOS, the edge rail and the menu bar panel size their own windows and refuse resizes from anywhere else, which fixes the crash that could close the app as the rail opened or closed (#1518, #1519, #1521).
- On macOS, the app uses the NEAR AI blue accent in place of purple, with clearer Home sections, Insights headers and refresh buttons (#1514, #1522).
- All three apps trust an updated set of root certificates: the emSign roots that are no longer trusted are gone (#1515, #1516, #1520).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1514, #1515, #1516, #1518, #1519, #1520, #1521 and #1522.
