# Near.ai v0.20.3

Insights now shows the sessions in your watched folders.

- On macOS, the Insights Sessions tab lists the sessions from your watched folders, by folder, time and tool, and draws each one turn by turn. No path is stored or shown (#1496).
- On macOS, the notice that today's upload limit is used up, and the other notices about traces waiting, can be closed. One that is closed comes back if its cause returns (#1495).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1495 and #1496.
