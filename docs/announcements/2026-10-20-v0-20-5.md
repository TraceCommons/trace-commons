# Near.ai v0.20.5

The macOS app no longer quits when the menu bar panel changes size.

- On macOS, the menu bar panel sizes its own window instead of leaving it to SwiftUI, which fixes a crash that could close the app while the panel was open (#1511).
- On macOS, VoiceOver lands on the Traces row you select, and on every row menu you open (#1506).
- On macOS, past sessions and Insights dates load faster with many sessions (#1507).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1506, #1507 and #1511.
