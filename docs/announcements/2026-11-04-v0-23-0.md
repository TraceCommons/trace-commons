# Near.ai v0.23.0

You can now move your account to a different NEAR AI login, on macOS, Windows and Linux.

## Sign in and account

- Use a different account, in the account section, moves your account to another NEAR AI login: you confirm the login you use now, sign in with the new one, then switch. Your credit, traces and payouts stay with your account (#1899, #1901, #1903).
- If you sign in with a NEAR AI login that belongs to another account, the app says so and offers Use a different account, instead of a generic failure (#1899, #1901, #1903).
- An account made with a NEAR wallet is offered Add a NEAR AI login, so its payouts can reach it (#1899, #1903).
- The change is offered only when the commons can take it (#1901).
- NEAR AI keys the app makes are named near-ai-app- in your NEAR AI key list (#1898).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1898, #1899, #1901 and #1903.
