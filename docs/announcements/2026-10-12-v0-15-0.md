# Near.ai v0.15.0

If you are signed in to NEAR AI, the Private AI tab now has "Start Claude Code" and "Start Codex". Pick a folder and the tool opens in your terminal, running on your NEAR AI sign-in. There is no account to set up and no key to paste.

- The two NEAR AI sessions appear on their own when you sign in and go away when you sign out. If you have not chosen a default account for a tool, NEAR AI is its default.
- Signing out ends any NEAR AI session that is still running.
- When you are not signed in, the same place offers "Sign in".

On macOS, the menu-bar panel no longer goes blank when one of its reads fails or its connection to the app's background service drops. Each value draws on its own, a value that could not be read says "Unknown", and the panel reconnects by itself.

Available on macOS and Windows. Changes include #1388 and #1389.
