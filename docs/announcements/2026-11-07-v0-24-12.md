# Near AI Gateway v0.24.12

For macOS and the contributor CLI. Windows and Linux desktop apps remain on their existing releases.

- After you switch accounts, sign out or unenroll, a late reply meant for the previous account can no longer replace the current account's history, and a late reply can no longer turn sharing back to Automatic after you chose Ask (#2017).
- Slow answers from the background service no longer tie up the app's shared worker threads, so several at once cannot stall the rest of the app (#2017).
- At launch the app reads your history and sharing choice once instead of twice (#2021).
