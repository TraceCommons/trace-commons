# Near.ai v0.24.10

For macOS and the contributor CLI. Windows and Linux desktop apps remain on their existing releases.

- Reviewing an ordinary trace no longer stops with "admission receipt unavailable" when your account supports admission evidence. A trace whose AI call is bound for admission still needs its receipt and your consent to share that call (#2013).
- Reviewed traces stay valid when the Commons privacy-check server moves to a new signing key under a signed update. A review is refused once its signing key is withdrawn or expires. A change to the server's published measurements alone does not make a review stale (#2013).

The existing requirements for token bundles, corrections and reviewed previews remain in place.
