# Near AI Gateway v0.24.14

For macOS and the contributor CLI. Windows and Linux desktop apps remain on their existing releases.

- Home has a new layout. Private AI comes first, then Traces, then Account. While Train coding models is off, a short line under the Traces heading says no credit is earned yet, with a button to open Settings (#2040).
- Home's getting-started card is now titled "Your traces earn Private AI credit". Each of its three steps sits in its own box and says a little more: keys, emails and file paths are removed before traces are sent, credit becomes final once it settles, and credit pays for Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode sessions through Private AI. It still closes with its X (#2040).
- The Insights card on Home has a new blue and no longer has an X. The guide on the Private AI tab uses the same getting-started card as Home (#2040).
- The first-run Welcome screen now asks you to accept the Terms of Service before you continue, with separate links to the Terms and the Privacy Policy (#2034).
- The browser sign-in page shows the GitHub, Google and NEAR logos beside their sign-in choices (#2035).
- Insights shows a Claude subscription once, instead of twice, when both readings belong to the same account (#2031).
- Updates the bundled SQLite to 3.54.1 to fix a write-ahead-log advisory (RUSTSEC-2026-0459) (#2041).
