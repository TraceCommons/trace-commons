# Near.ai v0.14.0

If you joined with an invite, every app now offers to move your contributions to a NEAR AI account, and sign-in to NEAR AI uses your NEAR wallet by default, so a passkey wallet works.

In every app:

- Moving an invite to a NEAR AI account. A notice offers the move, and Settings keeps it available if you choose "Dismiss". Moving signs you in to NEAR AI in the browser. An invite shared by many people, such as a hackathon invite, cannot be moved; the app says so once and stops offering. Available on macOS, Windows and Linux.
- Sign-in to NEAR AI starts with "Sign in with NEAR" rather than GitHub.
- More is removed from tool output before it leaves the device: git author names, IP addresses, private host names, account user names and phone numbers. Each becomes a numbered placeholder, so the same value reads the same way across a trace.

On macOS:

- The Traces tab has a heading, and its order control is an icon with a menu.
- Home's Missions, History and Insights cards share one layout, and Missions is highlighted.
- The edge rail has Home, Private AI, Traces and Compute tabs, with small maps of Private AI and Traces.
- The menu-bar panel sits directly under the menu bar, and its badge is smaller.
- In Light appearance the window background is back to the opacity it had before v0.13.1.
- The two Move buttons, on the notice and in Settings, have different names for screen readers.

Changes include #1366, #1368, #1370, #1372, #1375, #1377, #1380 and #1382.
