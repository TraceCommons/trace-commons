# Near.ai v0.20.1

This release fixes a hang that could stop uploads and approvals, and makes the macOS app lighter on CPU.

- Fixed a hang that could stop uploads, approvals and Insights while a large backlog was being sent, for example after switching a folder to Automatic (#1488).
- Claude Code sessions on NEAR AI no longer fail on their first message (#1483).
- From the command line, `launch` runs Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode on your NEAR AI sign-in by default and offers to sign you in if needed. Codex sessions on NEAR AI no longer fail on their first message (#1492).
- On macOS, the NEAR AI balance refreshes when you press its refresh button and when you come back from paying for credits (#1482, #1479).
- On macOS, the app uses far less CPU at rest, and the Traces tab keeps up when many traces are sent at once (#1485, #1484).
- On macOS, the map shows sessions on your NEAR AI sign-in going to NEAR AI, and notices open as cards over their own tab (#1486, #1481).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1479, #1481, #1482, #1483, #1484, #1485, #1486, #1488 and #1492.
