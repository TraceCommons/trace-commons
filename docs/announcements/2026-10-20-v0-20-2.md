# Near.ai v0.20.2

The macOS app's menu bar, Private AI tab and Home are simpler.

- On macOS, the menu bar shows what is worth a second look, pause and resume, and a button per coding tool that opens it on your NEAR AI sign-in (#1490).
- On macOS, Private AI starts a tool from one button and a folder you choose, and says plainly when a launch fails, a tool needs installing or there is no terminal (#1490).
- On macOS, Home shows your balance beside Credit, and the traces you haven't contributed in one card (#1490).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1490 and #1493.
