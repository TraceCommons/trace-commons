# Near.ai v0.20.0

Switching a folder to Automatic now sends the traces already waiting in it, and the macOS app gives Insights its own tab.

- Switching a folder to Automatic sends the traces already waiting in it, not just new ones. Traces held for your review stay held, and no trace is sent until it has been quiet for a day (#1473).
- On macOS, Insights is its own tab (Cmd-4), laid out to fit the main window at its default width (#1430).
- On macOS, the Traces map draws each folder once and shows each contribution as it is sent. The window can be made wider without the map, and the Credit tile no longer says when nothing changed (#1460).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1430, #1460 and #1473.
