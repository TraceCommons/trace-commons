# Near AI Gateway v0.24.13

For macOS and the contributor CLI. Windows and Linux desktop apps remain on their existing releases.

- Home's getting-started card now reads "Contribute traces. Earn credit. Use Private AI." In a narrow or short window it shrinks to one short row per step, so your stats stay in view, and it grows back when there is room. You can close it with its X (#2025, #2028).
- The "How Private AI works" card on the Private AI tab works the same way: it shrinks in a small window, and you can close it with its X (#2025).
- Home's stat cards no longer draw a large faded icon behind their numbers (#2024).
- The promotion cards on Home have solid dark backgrounds and pixel-art graphics. On the Private AI tab, session rows use the full width of their card, and the NEAR AI balance is a plain line under its title (#2026).
