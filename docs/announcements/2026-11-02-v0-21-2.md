# Near.ai v0.21.2

You can sign in to NEAR AI with GitHub, Google or a NEAR wallet wherever the app asks you to sign in.

- On macOS, the Sign in buttons on Home's Private AI card, in the NEAR AI notice, and on the Private AI balance and billing cards let you choose GitHub, Google or NEAR wallet. Before, they always started the NEAR wallet sign-in (#1827).
- On Linux, the balance row's Sign in offers the same choice, and the list of logins comes from the app's core (#1827).
- On Windows, the Sign in on the Private AI page's session card uses the login chosen on that page instead of always the NEAR wallet (#1827).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1827.
