# Near.ai v0.20.4

Insights now counts your long sessions, and the Traces tab stays quick with hundreds of folders.

- Insights counts sessions of any size. Long sessions used to show as undated with no token counts. Counting runs in the background, so new traces and the queue no longer wait for it (#1499, #1504).
- On macOS, Insights Sessions is one scrolling list of every session, newest first, each with its tokens and a sparkline. Click a session to see it turn by turn (#1502).
- On macOS, a Patterns card that rounds to zero tokens shows its count instead ("2 reads"). Analyze opens its file picker in your sessions folder (#1498).
- On macOS, the Traces tab opens, scrolls and responds to clicks much faster with many watched folders, and a trace or folder you act on shows that it is working right away (#1500, #1505).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1498, #1499, #1500, #1502, #1503, #1504 and #1505.
