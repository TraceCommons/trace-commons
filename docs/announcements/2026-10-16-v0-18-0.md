# Near.ai v0.18.0

This release prepares the macOS app to show what the traces on your machine could earn, and it hides missions for now.

- The macOS app can estimate the credit the traces on this machine could earn, in the menu bar and in a notification. The estimate appears once the service publishes the figures it is based on, and you can turn it off in Settings (#1431, #1445).
- Missions are hidden for now, in every app (#1443).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Changes include #1431, #1443 and #1445.
