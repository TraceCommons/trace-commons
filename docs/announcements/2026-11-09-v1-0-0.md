# Near AI Gateway 1.0

Near AI Gateway is a Mac app for people who work with AI coding agents. It keeps the record of that work under your control, lets you earn credit for sharing it, and can route your tools' AI requests through NEAR AI's private inference.

For macOS 14 or later, plus the contributor CLI. Windows and Linux desktop apps remain on their existing releases.

## What it does

- **Watches the folders you choose.** It finds the session transcripts that Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cline and OpenCode leave on your Mac, and reads only the folders you allow.
- **Keeps raw traces on your Mac.** Each trace is scrubbed on your computer before anything leaves it. Sharing is off until you turn it on, and you choose which uses your traces allow, such as evaluation or training coding models.
- **Earns credit for accepted traces.** Shared traces are checked on the server for whether they are new and substantive. Accepted ones earn Trace Credit, and first run shows what your traces could earn before you share.
- **Private AI.** Your tools can send their AI requests through the app, to be answered by NEAR AI after you sign in. No tool switches over until you connect it, and requests are logged on this Mac.
- **Insights.** A private view of your own agent work, kept on this Mac.

## Getting started

Install from the DMG on this release, or with `brew install --cask TraceCommons/tap/trace-commons`. First run walks through:

1. Choosing the tools to watch.
2. Connecting to Private AI.
3. Seeing what your traces could earn.
4. Choosing which data uses to allow.
5. Reading the Terms of Service.
6. Signing in with your NEAR AI account.

The current terms are a placeholder and not yet in effect.

Older pre-1.0 releases have been retired from this page. Installed apps update to 1.0 on their own.
