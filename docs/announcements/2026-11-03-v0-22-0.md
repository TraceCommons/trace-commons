# Near.ai v0.22.0

NEAR AI sign-in now lets you pick your login in the browser, Private AI sessions open in more terminals, and the app tells you when your account needs setting up again.

## Sign in and account

- Sign in opens a page in your browser that lists NEAR wallet, GitHub and Google. It replaces the menus the app showed before. The page lists the login you used last time first (#1835).
- If this app's account needs to be set up again, the account section says so and offers Set up again. This shows on macOS, Windows, Linux and the Tauri app (#1844, #1863).
- Signing out of your account also signs you out of NEAR AI on this computer (#1843, #1860).
- Cancelling a NEAR AI sign-in no longer reads as a failure (#1858, #1860).
- A NEAR AI sign-in checks the account it belongs to on a device that was signed out, and a failed sign-in no longer drops the link between your NEAR AI login and your account (#1837, #1840).
- When the commons is busy or cannot serve a sign-in, the app says it is unavailable instead of refusing you (#1850, #1856, #1864).

## Private AI sessions and terminals

- The launch dialog asks which terminal to open in every time, when more than one is installed (#1890).
- Warp is a terminal choice on macOS. Sessions open as tabs in Warp with no permission prompt (#1870, #1871, #1887).
- Rex is a terminal choice on macOS. Sessions open as tabs in a Rex session named Near.ai (#1886).
- On macOS and Linux, a Group sessions in herdr switch opens sessions as tabs in a herdr workspace named Near.ai. The app says what went wrong when herdr's socket is not trusted or Rex is slow (#1886, #1892).
- Opening a Private AI session no longer asks macOS for permission. On macOS each Terminal launch opens a window of its own (#1867, #1869).
- On macOS, clicking a running session shows its terminal. Start again and Resume open a new window, and a resumed conversation is listed once and can be dismissed after a failed resume (#1838, #1839, #1846, #1849, #1853).
- Managed Claude Code sessions no longer ask for server classifier review, and a project cannot turn it back on (#1851).

## Also on macOS

- Drag folders on the Traces tab into your own order. The order menu has a Your order choice, and each folder has Move up and Move down actions. Dragging a folder no longer moves the window (#1830, #1831, #1834).
- Home shows your account's total credit earned under this Mac's figure. Insights drops Show week and the Higher-than-usual card switch. Getting-started cards look the same on Insights, Private AI and Home (#1888).
- Screens and messages say Near.ai instead of the old product name, and says when the commons answered unusably instead of not answering (#1854, #1857).

Available on macOS, Windows and Linux. Terminal choices are on macOS; herdr is on macOS and Linux.
